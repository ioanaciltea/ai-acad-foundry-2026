# Customer Security Guidelines & Cyber Protection

Libra Bank maintains strict cyber security standards to safeguard customer identity and financial assets.

## 1. Official Communication Policy

Libra Bank staff will **NEVER**:
* Request your 4-digit Card PIN, 3-digit CVV/CVC code, or internet banking password over phone, email, or SMS.
* Send SMS messages containing direct login links requiring credential input.
* Ask you to install remote access applications (such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer) to fix account issues.

## 2. Recognizing Phishing & Smishing

* **Web URL Verification**: Always verify that the web banking URL starts with `https://ib.librabank.ro` and exhibits a valid TLS security certificate.
* **Fake Urgency**: Be cautious of emails or calls claiming your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless you verify credentials.

## 3. Emergency Procedure for Compromised Accounts

If you suspect your credentials or card details have been compromised:
1. Immediately block your card via the Libra Mobile App (go to `Cards` -> `Select Card` -> `Block Card`).
2. Call the **24/7 Security Emergency Hotline**: **0800-800-999** (toll-free in Romania) or **+40 21 208 8000** (international).
3. Change your online banking password immediately from an uncompromised device.
