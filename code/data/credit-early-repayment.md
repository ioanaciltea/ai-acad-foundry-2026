# Early Repayment of Card Credit

Customers who have an active credit facility on their Libra Bank credit card can make early repayments, partial or total, at any time, without minimum period restrictions.

The early repayment fee is 1% of the early repaid amount, applied once, regardless of whether the repayment is partial or total. This fee is identical for all credit card types (Standard, Gold, Premium).

Calculation example: for an early repayment of 5,000 RON, the fee charged is 50 RON (1% of 5,000 RON).

Early repayment can be made from the Libra Assist app, under "My Credits" -> "Repay early", or at any Libra Bank branch. The minimum amount for a partial early repayment is 100 RON.
