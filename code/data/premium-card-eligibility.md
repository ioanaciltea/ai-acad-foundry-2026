# Eligibility Conditions for the Premium Card

The Libra Bank Premium Card is available only to customers who simultaneously meet all of the following conditions:

1. Net monthly income collected into a Libra Bank account of at least 8,000 RON, for the last 3 consecutive months.
2. Tenure of at least 12 months as a Libra Bank customer, with at least one active product (current account, savings, or credit).
3. No overdue payments on any credit facility in the last 6 months.
4. Minimum age of 21 years.

The Premium Card application is submitted online or at a branch and is reviewed within a maximum of 5 business days. Approval is not automatic; the risk department may request additional documents (income certificate, bank statement from another bank).

For the effective costs of the Premium Card (issuance fee, annual maintenance, withdrawal fees), consult the document "Card fee list - 2026 edition".
