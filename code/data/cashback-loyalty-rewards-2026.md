# Libra Rewards & Cashback Program (2026 Edition)

The Libra Rewards program automatically credits cash back to cardholders for eligible POS and online debit and credit card payments.

## 1. Cashback Tiers & Categories

* **Standard Card**: 0.5% cashback on supermarket and fuel transactions.
* **Active & Gold Card**: 1.0% cashback on supermarket, fuel, and restaurant transactions.
* **Premium Card**: 2.0% cashback on partner travel merchants (airline tickets, hotels) and 1.0% on all other eligible retail merchant spending.

## 2. Program Rules & Caps

1. **Monthly Cashback Cap**: Maximum 200 RON credited per customer per calendar month.
2. **Excluded Transactions**: ATM cash withdrawals, wire transfers, gambling/betting transactions, tax payments, and utility bill payments do not earn cashback.
3. **Payout Timing**: Cashback accumulated during a calendar month is automatically transferred into the customer's main current account on the 5th day of the following month.
