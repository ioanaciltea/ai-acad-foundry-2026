# Cards for Business Clients

Libra Bank offers two types of cards for legal entities: Business Card and Business Gold Card, both issued in the company's name with one or more authorized users.

The issuance fee for a Business Card is 60 RON per card, and for a Business Gold Card it is 100 RON per card. The annual maintenance is 150 RON for the Business Card and 400 RON for the Business Gold Card.

The daily transaction limit for a Business Card is 10,000 RON, and for a Business Gold Card it is 30,000 RON. The company can request customized limits per authorized user through the dedicated account manager.

Business cards are not available for individuals and cannot be linked to a personal current account.
