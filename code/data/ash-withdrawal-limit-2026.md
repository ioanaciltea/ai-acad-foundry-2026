# Daily Cash Withdrawal Limit (valid starting January 1, 2026)

Starting January 1, 2026, the standard daily ATM cash withdrawal limit for the Standard Card was increased to 3,000 RON per day.

For the Gold Card, the limit is 6,000 RON per day, and for the Premium Card, the limit is 15,000 RON per day.

Temporary limit increases can still be requested via the Call Center or from the Libra Assist app, up to a maximum of 10,000 RON per day, for a maximum of 30 days, regardless of the card type.
