# Products that Libra Bank Does NOT Offer

For clarity towards customers and partners, Libra Bank does NOT currently offer the following products and services:

1. Student loans (student borrowings) - Libra Bank has no student loan product in its portfolio, regardless of the country of study or university level.
2. Cryptocurrencies - the bank does not offer cryptocurrency wallets, does not facilitate cryptocurrency transactions, and does not provide custody for digital assets.
3. Unit-linked life insurance (investment-linked life insurance) - these are not in the current portfolio, although the bank collaborates with external partners for classical life insurance.

Customers asking about these products must be directed to other financial institutions or, in the case of insurance, to the bank's external partners.
