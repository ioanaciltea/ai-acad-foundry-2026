# Savings Accounts and Term Deposits Rates (2026)

Libra Bank offers a range of flexible savings products and term deposits for individual retail clients.

## 1. Savings Products Overview

| Product Name | Interest Rate (p.a.) | Minimum Amount | Liquidity / Term | Penalty for Early Withdrawal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Libra Flexi-Savings | 3.50% | 100 RON | Instant / No term | None (interest calculated daily) |
| Deposit 3 Months | 5.25% | 1,000 RON | 3 Months | Total loss of accrued interest |
| Deposit 6 Months | 5.80% | 1,000 RON | 6 Months | Total loss of accrued interest |
| Deposit 12 Months | 6.20% | 1,000 RON | 12 Months | Total loss of accrued interest |

## 2. Key Terms & Conditions

1. **Flexi-Savings Account**: Interest is accrued daily based on end-of-day balance and credited monthly into the current account on the 1st business day of each month.
2. **Term Deposits**: At maturity, deposits can automatically roll over for an identical term at the interest rate in effect on the renewal date, unless non-renewal instructions are set in the mobile app.
3. **Tax Withholding**: According to Romanian fiscal law, a 10% tax on interest income is automatically withheld at source by Libra Bank for resident retail clients.
4. **Deposit Guarantee**: All retail deposits are guaranteed by the Deposit Guarantee Fund in Banking (FGDB) up to the equivalent of 100,000 EUR per depositor.
