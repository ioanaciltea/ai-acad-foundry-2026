# SME & Corporate Credit Facilities

Libra Bank provides tailored working capital and revolving credit line solutions to Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) and corporate entities registered in Romania.

## 1. Product Characteristics

* **Facility Type**: Revolving Working Capital Line (Linie de Credit Revolving).
* **Maximum Limit**: Up to 500,000 RON (unsecured for qualifying SMEs) or up to 2,500,000 RON (secured with real estate or FNGCIMM guarantees).
* **Interest Rate Structure**: Variable rate calculated as `ROBOR 3M + 4.50%` per annum.
* **Management & Commitment Fees**:
  * Credit approval fee: 0.50% one-off.
  * Annual renewal fee: 0.50% of approved credit limit.
  * Non-utilization fee: 0.00% (no penalty for unutilized limit).

## 2. Company Eligibility Criteria

1. Minimum **12 months of active business operations** with positive net turnover.
2. Positive operational profit (EBITDA > 0) in the last full fiscal year.
3. No active litigation with the Ministry of Finance / ANAF and valid tax clearance certificate (certificat de atestare fiscală).
4. Major shareholders/administrators must have no negative records in the CRC (Centrala Riscului de Credit).

## 3. Required Financial Documents

1. Annual Financial Statements (Bilanț contabil) for the last 2 closed fiscal years.
2. Trial balance (Balanță de verificare) for the most recent month.
3. Corporate resolution approving the credit request.
