# Card Blocking and Unblocking

A Libra Bank card is automatically blocked by the system in the following situations:

1. The incorrect PIN is entered 3 (three) consecutive times at an ATM or POS.
2. The customer requests temporary blocking from the Libra Assist mobile app.
3. The fraud department detects a suspicious transaction (e.g., an unusual payment abroad).
4. The customer reports the card lost or stolen via the Call Center.

## Steps for Unblocking

To unblock a card blocked due to an incorrect PIN, the customer must follow these exact steps, in this order:

1. Log into the Libra Assist app using username and password.
2. Access the "My Cards" section and select the blocked card.
3. Tap the "Request Unblock" button.
4. Confirm identity through an OTP code sent to the registered phone number.
5. Enter a new 4-digit PIN, different from the last 3 PINs used.
6. The system confirms the unblocking within a maximum of 2 minutes; the card can be used immediately after this confirmation.

If the blocking was initiated by the fraud department (point 3), unblocking cannot be done from the app. The customer must contact the Libra Bank Call Center at 021-9-LIBRA, available 24/7, and verbally confirm the last 3 transactions on the card.

Cards reported lost or stolen (point 4) can no longer be unblocked; they are permanently canceled, and the customer must request a replacement card (see document "Card Replacement").
