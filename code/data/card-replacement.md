# Card Replacement

The card replacement procedure applies in three situations: lost card, stolen card, or expired card. The steps differ slightly depending on the reason.

## Standard Steps (lost or stolen card)

1. The customer immediately calls the Libra Bank Call Center (021-9-LIBRA) and reports the incident.
2. The operator permanently cancels the old card within a maximum of 5 minutes from the call.
3. The customer confirms the delivery address or chooses pickup from the nearest branch.
4. A new card issuance request is completed, also available in the Libra Assist app, under "My Cards" -> "Request new card".
5. The new card is produced in 3 business days and delivered by courier in 2-5 business days, or is available at the branch in 3 business days.
6. The new PIN is sent separately via a secure SMS after confirming card delivery.
7. The old card, if found later, can no longer be reactivated under any circumstances.

## Steps for Expired Card

Expired cards are automatically renewed, without customer request, 30 days before the expiration date. The customer receives the new card by courier at the registered address; no action is required unless they wish to change the delivery address.

## Associated Fees

Issuing a replacement card for a lost or stolen card costs 50 RON. Renewal upon expiration is free of charge.
