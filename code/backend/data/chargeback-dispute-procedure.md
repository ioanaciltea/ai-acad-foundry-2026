# Merchant Transaction Disputes & Chargeback Procedure

This document outlines the step-by-step procedure for cardholders to contest unauthorized, erroneous, or unfulfilled merchant card transactions.

## 1. Valid Dispute Grounds

* Duplicate charging for a single purchase.
* Incorrect transaction amount billed.
* Paid by alternative payment method (cash/other card), but card was charged.
* Goods or services ordered online were never delivered or rendered.
* Cancelled recurring subscription still billed after cancellation confirmation.

## 2. Step-by-Step Dispute Process

1. **Attempt Merchant Resolution**: First attempt to contact the merchant directly to request a refund.
2. **Gather Evidence**: Collect receipts, cancellation emails, proof of return shipping, or merchant correspondence.
3. **Submit Official Form**: Complete the *Libra Card Dispute Declaration Form* via the mobile app under `Card Services` -> `Dispute Transaction`.
4. **Bank Submission**: Submit the form and documents within **60 calendar days** from the transaction posting date.
5. **Investigation & Resolution**: The dispute team processes claims under Visa/Mastercard scheme rules. Provisional credit may be granted within 10 business days for clear merchant errors. Final resolution takes up to **45 business days**.
