# Interest and Costs of Card Credit

The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) for Libra Bank credit cards is 19.9% per year, for all credit card types.

Customers benefit from a 45-day interest-free grace period if the balance is paid in full before the due date displayed on the account statement. If the balance is not paid in full, the 19.9% interest applies retroactively to the entire utilized amount, from the date of each transaction.

The mandatory minimum monthly payment is 5% of the outstanding balance, but not less than 50 RON.

For the cost of early repayment of this credit, see the document "Early repayment of card credit".
