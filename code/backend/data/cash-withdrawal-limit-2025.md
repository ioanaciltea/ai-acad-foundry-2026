# Daily Cash Withdrawal Limit (valid June 2025 - December 2025)

The standard daily ATM cash withdrawal limit for the Standard Card was 2,000 RON per day, during the period from June 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025.

This limit could be temporarily increased upon request via the Call Center up to a maximum of 5,000 RON per day, for a maximum period of 30 days.

This version has been replaced as of January 1, 2026. Consult the document "Daily cash withdrawal limit - 2026 version" for current values.
