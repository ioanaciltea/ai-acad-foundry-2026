# International Transfers and SEPA Payments Procedure

This document governs incoming and outgoing international wire transfers, SEPA payments, and foreign currency exchange for retail customers of Libra Bank.

## 1. SEPA Transfers (Single Euro Payments Area)

* **Eligible Currencies**: EUR only.
* **Execution Timeframe**: Standard SEPA transfers submitted before 14:30 EET are executed same-day (T+0); transfers submitted after 14:30 are processed next business day (T+1). Instant SEPA transfers execute within 10 seconds 24/7.
* **Fees (Online Banking)**: 0 RON for standard SEPA payments up to 10,000 EUR. 5 RON flat fee for amounts exceeding 10,000 EUR.
* **Fees (Branch)**: 15 RON flat fee for any SEPA transfer initiated at a physical branch.

## 2. Non-SEPA / SWIFT International Wire Transfers

* **Supported Currencies**: USD, GBP, CHF, CAD, JPY.
* **Cut-Off Time**: 15:30 EET for same-day dispatch.
* **Fee Structure**:
  * SHA (Shared): Commission 0.15% (min 15 EUR, max 200 EUR) paid by sender; intermediary bank fees deducted from payout.
  * OUR (All fees paid by sender): Commission 0.15% + flat 25 EUR intermediary cover fee.

## 3. Required Payment Data

To guarantee processing without delay, senders must specify:
1. Full beneficiary name and physical address.
2. Beneficiary IBAN (for SEPA) or full account number (for non-SEPA).
3. SWIFT / BIC code of receiving institution.
4. Payment reference details (invoice number, purpose of transfer).
