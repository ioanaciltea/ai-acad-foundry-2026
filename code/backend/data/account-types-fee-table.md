# Comparative Fee Table by Account Type

The table below centralizes the monthly maintenance fees for each type of current account offered by Libra Bank. This table must not be fragmented, as the values only make sense together with the account type from the same row.

| Account Type | Monthly Maintenance Fee | Free Included Card | Free Internal Transfers/Month | Paper Bank Statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start Account | 0 RON | Standard Card | 5 | 10 RON/month |
| Active Account | 9.9 RON | Standard Card | 20 | Free |
| Premium Account | 25 RON | Gold Card | Unlimited | Free |
| Business Basic Account | 39 RON | Business Card | 30 | Free |
| Business Pro Account | 89 RON | Business Gold Card | Unlimited | Free |

All values are expressed excluding VAT, where applicable. The Start Account does not allow overdrafts under any circumstances.
