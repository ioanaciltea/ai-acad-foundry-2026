# Card Fee List (Valid starting 2026)

This is the current list of fees, in effect from January 1, 2026, replacing the 2025 edition.

| Fee Type | Standard Card | Gold Card | Premium Card |
|---|---|---|---|
| New card issuance | 25 RON | 45 RON | 0 RON |
| Annual maintenance | 72 RON | 132 RON | 300 RON |
| Cash withdrawal at another ATM (domestic) | 1.2% (min. 4 RON) | 0.6% (min. 3 RON) | 0 RON |
| Replacement card (lost/stolen) | 50 RON | 50 RON | 0 RON |
| Cash withdrawal abroad | 2.2% | 1.7% | 0.5% |

Main changes compared to 2025: annual maintenance increased by approximately 10% for all card types, and the replacement card fee increased from 40 RON to 50 RON.
