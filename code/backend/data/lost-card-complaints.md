# Complaint Procedure for Unauthorized Transactions

If a customer notices a card transaction they did not authorize, they must follow these steps:

1. Immediately block the card from the Libra Assist app or via the Call Center.
2. File a written complaint, online or at a branch, within a maximum of 13 business days from the date the transaction was noticed.
3. The complaint must include: transaction date, amount, merchant (if known), and a statement that the transaction was not authorized.
4. Libra Bank investigates the complaint within a maximum of 15 business days and communicates the result in writing.
5. If the complaint is confirmed as fraud, the amount is refunded to the customer within no more than 24 hours from the completion of the investigation, in accordance with payment services regulations.

Complaints filed after the 13-business-day deadline may be analyzed, but reimbursement is no longer guaranteed by regulation and depends on the bank's decision.
