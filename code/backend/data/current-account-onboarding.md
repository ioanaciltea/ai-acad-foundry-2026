# Opening a Current Account - Onboarding

A new customer can open a Libra Bank current account in two ways: online, through the Libra Assist app, or at a branch.

## Online Onboarding

1. Download the Libra Assist app and choose "Open new account".
2. Enter personal details and take a photo of the identity card (front and back).
3. Perform a video identity verification (liveness check), which takes approximately 3 minutes.
4. Choose the account type (Start, Active, or Premium) and, if eligible, the associated card type.
5. The account is active within a maximum of 10 minutes from the completion of the video verification, and the physical card is delivered by courier in 3-5 business days.

## Branch Onboarding

The customer visits any Libra Bank branch with their ID card, completes the account opening form, and receives the physical card on the spot, in approximately 20 minutes, assuming that the respective card is in stock at the branch.

The minimum age to open an individual current account is 18 years.
