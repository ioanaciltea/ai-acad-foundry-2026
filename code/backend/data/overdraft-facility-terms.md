# Overdraft Facility Terms and Conditions

An overdraft is a revolving credit facility linked directly to a Libra Bank current account, allowing customers to spend beyond their zero balance up to a pre-approved credit limit.

## 1. Credit Limits & Interest Rates

* **Maximum Credit Limit**: Up to 3 times the net monthly salary collected into Libra Bank (maximum 30,000 RON).
* **Interest Rate**: 14.50% per annum (variable, calculated daily on the drawn amount).
* **One-Time Granting Fee**: 50 RON deducted upon line activation.
* **Annual Renewal Fee**: 0 RON.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

1. Minimum 3 consecutive months of salary / recurring income indexed to a Libra Bank Active or Premium account.
2. Active employment contract with remaining duration of at least 6 months.
3. No active negative entries in the Credit Bureau (Biroul de Credit).
4. **Exclusions**: The **Start Account** type is strictly excluded from overdraft approval.

## 3. Repayment Dynamics

* Monthly interest accrued is automatically debited on the last day of each calendar month from incoming salary funds.
* There is no fixed monthly principal installment, but incoming transfers automatically reduce the utilized overdraft balance.
